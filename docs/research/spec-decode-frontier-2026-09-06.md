# Speculative-decoding frontier for the Qwen3.8-27B stack, surveyed 2026-09-06

Every claim below was read on a page fetched this session; dates are the source's. UNVERIFIED marks what no fetched page confirmed. The baseline for "worth" is arc three: the shipped DFlash2 head at width 7 gives about 3.8 tokens per step at 23.2 ms per step on a 4090 (W4A16 target). Ranking is expected throughput gain per day of work.

## Ranked candidates

**1. The warm-start fine-tune, with three levers that appeared since the probe.** (a) Verification-Aware Training, arXiv 2608.30135 (2026-08-31): a jointly trained verification head plus position weights re-anchored at each sample's first rejection; on DFlash with Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.1-8B it reports acceptance length up to +11.4% and wall-clock up to +8.7%, training objective only. The code URL in the abstract (github.com/naver-ai/vat) returned 404 today. (b) SpecForge PR #831 (2026-09-03, open) adds `model.target_head_path` so the trainer reads a bf16 head beside a quantized target; validated on Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 (https://github.com/sgl-project/SpecForge/pull/831). (c) SpecForge merged structured DFlash2 training metrics on 2026-09-02 and 03: expected accepted length per block and a tracker per block position (https://github.com/sgl-project/SpecForge/commits/main). Applies to a hybrid, quantized target: yes, that is the probe already run. Cost: 3 to 5 days plus the 33 to 43 GPU-hours the probe priced. Worth: the official card reports 4.80 tokens per step at block 8 on a bf16 target, H200, SGLang (https://huggingface.co/z-lab/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2); ours is 3.83 on W4A16. If half that gap is target mismatch, +10 to +15% throughput. Risk: the export round trip is unproven; AdaFlash, arXiv 2607.19223 (2026-07-21), argues diffusion drafters need on-policy reverse-KL distillation to tame domain variance, no code. Verdict: best gain per day on the list; run item 2 first.

**2. The acceptance-histogram preflight (hours).** arXiv 2608.30427 (2026-08-31, DBloom): plot the per-cycle accepted-length histogram; a spike in the ceiling bin (whole block accepted) means a wider block would pay, otherwise it will not, and widening at inference without retraining "erodes front-of-block verification". Expanding DFlash from block 16 to 24 on Qwen3-8B with a short curriculum gave +0.8 tokens median. Arc three's 1% for width 15 predicts a small ceiling bin. Verdict: compute it from the existing ROW lines; it settles the width question with numbers and costs nothing.

**3. Head precision check (1 day).** thomasgardiner/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2-FP8 (2026-09-06) casts 20 MLP and attention-out tensors of the incoai head to FP8: acceptance −1.3% to +0.8%, decode +4.0% to +5.8% against the bf16 head, RTX 5090, SGLang, NVFP4 target, 8 draft tokens. z-lab/dflash issue #160 (2026-08-19, MLX) reports a 4-bit head with fp16 activations collapsing to 6.25% acceptance where w4a32 gives 75%: MLX-specific, but it says the selector is precision-sensitive. Our head is W4A16. Try the bf16 (3.85 GB) or FP8 (2.41 GB) head at the reduced profile that served the 3.5 GiB DSpark head, and compare acceptance. Worth 0 to +5%. Risk: the memory ladder.

**4. Tensor parallel across both 4090s (adjacent to the question, 1 day).** A community post reports 218 tok/s single request on two RTX 3090s at TP=2 over PCIe Gen4 with patched P2P, AutoRound INT4, DFlash2 with 7 drafts and acceptance 3.35, against 120.1 tok/s without speculation, vLLM 0.26.1rc1 plus patches (https://theneuralfeed.com/article/qwen3-8-27b-on-2x-3090-vllm-dflash2-218-tok-s-single-request/dpvyXgFy, 2026-08-19); club-3090 discussion 1076 (2026-08-21) reports 128 and 231 tok/s (narrative, code) on the same pair at vLLM 0.27.1. Ours: 150 on one 3090, 160 on one 4090. Risk: WSL2 has no P2P, so NCCL falls back to host copies and 64 layers of all-reduce may eat the halved weight read; it also takes card 1 from training. UNVERIFIED on this host. Cost: one flag, one boot.

**5. Multi-turn prefix caching check (half a day; time to first token, not per step).** vLLM issue #54360 (2026-08-29): on nightly v0.28.1rc1.dev43, Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 on a GB10, an 18,219-token prompt sent twice: caching alone took TTFT from 9.46 s to 0.64 s with 17,248 hits; with DFlash k=7 it went 12.9 s to 12.5 s with 0 hits; v0.24.0 worked. PR #53802 (2026-08-25, open) measured 97.7 to 98.6% hits with DFlash2 on the same target on another build. Read `prefix_cache_hits_total` on the fork; if it is zero, every turn pays a full prefill.

**6. Block verification (hours, zero code).** `rejection_sample_method=block` is in 0.28; the method's paper reports 5% to 8% wall-clock over token verification (arXiv 2403.10444). It moves only under sampling; under greedy it is identical. Not in the record as tried. Worth 0 to 5% on sampled workloads.

## Not worth days here, with the reason

- **ReplaySSM** (Dao AI Lab), vLLM RFCs #47572 (2026-07-03) and #49232 (2026-07-20). Standard decode at batch 1: 0.98x to 0.99x on Qwen3.5-4B (H100) and Qwen3.5-122B (B300); with an MTP drafter at (B,T)=(16,4), 0.99x at batch 1 and 1.80x at 256 on the 122B. Only the Mamba2 FlashInfer backend has merged (PR #52506, 2026-09-01); the GDN spec kernel PR #49887 (2026-07-26) is open and MTP-only. Nothing at batch 1.
- **TreeWY**, arXiv 2608.20961 (2026-08-21) and vLLM RFC #54080 (2026-08-27): a fork branch, one commit dated 2026-08-08, 33 files, MTP chains on Qwen3.5 35B and 397B on B200. Acceptance rises from 1.883 to 2.786 between (1,1,1) and (3,3,3) trees but "throughput falls at every width", because a real tree needs a non-causal mask that drops the model to piecewise CUDA graphs and evicts the GDN mixer. Its win is memory at 256 concurrency, and "a few percent" cost where memory does not bind. Bole, arXiv 2608.01651 (2026-08-03, SGLang), claims 3.4x to 7.7x faster linear-attention tree verify with no code linked. Tree verification for GDN hybrids now exists on paper; nowhere is it a batch-1 throughput win.
- **Adaptive verification in vLLM** (`enable_adaptive_verification`, PR #47808 merged 2026-08-12) is DSpark-only per the docs; PR #53929 (open) for Qwen GDN shows static K=8 faster at concurrency 1 to 16. LibraSpec, arXiv 2608.08721 (2026-08-09, training-free dynamic length for diffusion drafters, up to 8.49x), and the AdaFlash length head save verify cost, which arc three measured flat in K at batch 1 (23.2 ms at width 7, 23.4 at 15 once resident). Nothing at batch 1.
- **Rerankers and refiners that need a trained module.** LiLiCorr, arXiv 2608.20530 (2026-08-20): +9% to 19% acceptance on DFlash, SGLang PR #37462 (2026-09-01, open) says "Checkpoints are not yet published", trained on Qwen3-8B; DFlash2's selector already scores adjacent candidates (inco blog 2026-08-18: over 20% more output per pass for about 1% latency), so it is largely the same idea. xPress, arXiv 2608.02438 (+30% acceptance on Qwen3-8B DFlash, no code). ReTrace, arXiv 2608.29748 (no code).
- **Tree drafters.** DARTree, arXiv 2608.13524 (12.97 tokens per round on Qwen3-4B GSM8K, HF Transformers, needs a Domino head and tree attention); JetSpec, arXiv 2606.18394 (own vLLM fork with Triton tree attention, heads for six models, none Qwen3.8, no hybrid statement); CURE, PRESTO, GRAFT, DominoTree. All need tree verification vLLM main does not have (yesterday's survey: PR #42121 removed it on 2026-05-09; today's main-branch config still has no tree field).
- **Lossy verifiers.** Approximate SD, arXiv 2608.03447 (+3% to 15% throughput on Qwen3-14B with DSpark; "does not preserve the target model's greedy output"); FLy, vLLM PR #53987 (open, AMD, "explicitly lossy"). Excluded.
- **Engine swap to SGLang Spec V2.** An RTX 5090 comparison at 32K context, NVFP4 (https://suhailmohebi.com/insights/rtx-5090-llm-serving/, 2026-08-28): SGLang DFlash2 139.7 tok/s, vLLM native MTP 100.8; vLLM DFlash2 was not in it. UNVERIFIED that SGLang beats the fork's vLLM.
- **Oilbird**, arXiv 2608.03839 (2026-08-04): training-free semantic re-keying of the lookup pool, +24% to 29% accepted length on tool-calling traffic; the fork's lookup tail earns 0.6% on this cohort, so it is workload-bound. No code. The fork's own issue #38 (2026-08-24, closed) already covers lookup chains: 149 tok/s prose, 231 to 243 verbatim.

- A SpecForge Qwen3.8 config (the configs directory today still has none; nearest is qwen3.6-27b-dflash2.json) and any batch-1 consumer-GPU number for ReplaySSM, TreeWY or Bole (H100, B200, B300 only).
- Any lossless training-free method with a measured batch-1 gain on a block drafter.

New DFlash-family Qwen3.8-27B checkpoints since 2026-08-16 (community, HF): onewhosighs/Apathy-Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash-drafter-v3 (block 16, bf16 target, 72.2 tok/s on a GB10), rwmacy/qwen3.8-27b-dflash-drafter-fp8-b70 (Intel B70, from the RadixArk DSpark head), mrchuy bootstrap GGUF, HermiHg Q2_K GGUF.
