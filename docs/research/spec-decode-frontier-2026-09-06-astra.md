# Speculative decoding frontier, 6 September 2026

Research complete within the time box. Sources were fetched and read on 6 September. The [prior survey](spec-decode-alternatives-qwen38-2026-09-05.md) and relevant fork code were checked; the parallel investigator's report was not read. Baseline from the brief: Qwen3.8-27B W4A16, 48 GDN and 16 full-attention layers, vLLM 0.28.0 plus fork patches, five-layer DFlash2 block 8, about 3.8 committed tokens per step including bonus, single user. All day estimates are engineering judgments for small experiments on the two 4090s. **Every projected local gain is UNVERIFIED.** Ranking weighs likely gain, integration cost and evidence quality.

1. **Trim DFlash2's draft vocabulary.** The [memra card](https://huggingface.co/tiyuvta/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2-memra), modified 30 August, reports identical Inco weights with runtime vocabulary trimming. Qwen3.8-27B quantized target, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, q4 drafter, greedy, concurrency 1, three interleaved repetitions: limiting draft scoring to 32,768 rows raises chat throughput 136.3 to 142.9 tok/s and agentic throughput 154.5 to 157.2. Target verification retains the full vocabulary. Hybrid and quantized serving are demonstrated, in another engine. The fork's [DFlash2 projection](../../patches/dflash2-backport.patch) still scores the full head; its existing vocabulary slice serves MTP. Estimate **1-2 days** to repack selected quantized head rows, preserve token IDs through the selector and check rejection semantics. Risk: coverage loss reduces acceptance, especially multilingual. **Verdict: first experiment; modest matched gains and a concrete local opening.**

2. **Try DaoCloud's seven-query checkpoint.** [Card](https://huggingface.co/DaoCloud/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2-Exp), modified 31 August: `sample_from_anchor` obtains seven drafts from seven query positions. Qwen3.8-27B, H200, TP1, concurrency 1: HumanEval accepted-plus-bonus length 3.3457 with thinking at temperature 1, 6.5176 without thinking at 0.7; sampling filters also differ. There is no matched timing win over our checkpoint. Required [vLLM PR 54154](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/54154), opened 28 August, supports only Model Runner V2. Hybrid demonstrated; W4A16 target/draft transfer UNVERIFIED. Estimate **1-3 days** for query-layout compatibility, draft quantization and matched A/B, longer if runner migration is needed. Risk: residency or migration cost exceeds saved draft work. **Verdict: bounded compatibility probe, then a checkpoint comparison.**

3. **Remove redundant host metadata work selectively.** Open [vLLM PR 54485](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/54485), 31 August, skips unused DFlash/DSpark metadata rebuilds during full CUDA graph replay in Model Runner V2 and simplifies uniform hybrid decode metadata. On GB200, Nemotron-3.5-Lightning with DSpark, batch 1, the draft rebuild costs 0.125 ms per cycle before versus 0.007 after. Its broader 690 to 753-760 tok/s GB300 result uses synthetic acceptance 3.00 and bundles unrelated changes. Hybrid applicability is direct; weight quantization is orthogonal. Estimate **half a day to profile, 1-2 days to port** if the work exists on our runner. Risk: backend planning has necessary side effects; the skip currently opts in only safe Triton builders. Blackwell-only Marlin changes do not engage on a 4090. **Verdict: profile this specific overhead before porting.**

4. **DBloom: train beyond the block boundary only when acceptance reaches it.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30427v1), 31 August, expands pretrained DFlash/DFlare from block 16 to 24 using continuation training and a 30,000-prompt corpus. Qwen3-8B DFlash, seven-benchmark prompt-mean committed length: 6.54 to 7.02, mean relative speedup +5%; naive expansion yields 6.27 and -4%. Hardware UNVERIFIED. Hybrid, quantized features and our shorter DFlash2 block are untested, but the method leaves chain verification intact. Estimate **half a day** for a full-acceptance histogram, **3-5 days** for a small continuation probe. Risk: wider verification costs more and training can damage early acceptance. **Verdict: measure the ceiling first; the current mean alone does not justify widening.**

5. **LiLiCorr: a jointly trained parallel candidate scorer.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20530v1), 20 August; [NVIDIA blog](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/lilicorr/), 25 August; draft [SGLang PR 37462](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/37462), 1 September. A small Transformer scores the candidate lattice once, followed by a cheap greedy walk. Qwen3-8B, single H100, block 16, concurrency 1, greedy MT-Bench: vanilla DFlash 398 tok/s versus LiLiCorr 436; Domino 423, DSpark 427. Training used roughly 1.4 million target-regenerated examples for six epochs. Hybrid/W4A16 transfer is plausible because verification stays unchanged, but UNVERIFIED. Estimate **3-5 days for a small adaptation**, full training duration UNVERIFIED; no compatible weights found. Risk: the paper does not compare with DFlash2's existing selector. **Verdict: useful selector research, low confidence of an incremental win here.**

6. **Use the actual SGLang DFlash2 tree prototype as a feasibility test.** Draft [PR 36196](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/36196), 24 August, retains multiple selector paths and implements hybrid state/KV compaction. Qwen3.8-27B, single GPU of UNVERIFIED type, four concurrent requests, greedy, decode graphs disabled: width 1 gives acceptance length 3.42 and 132-142 tok/s; width 4 gives 4.39 and 172. Recurrent workspace rises 2.81 to 10.20 GB. Sampling is rejected; ReplaySSM is incompatible with branched verification. Quantized-target behavior is UNVERIFIED. Estimate **2-4 days for an isolated SGLang probe**, over a week for a dependable vLLM port. Risk: memory, missing graph capture and unresolved branch numerics. **Verdict: concrete hybrid code now exists, but these timings do not establish a single-user win.**

7. **Bole: reduce hybrid tree verification and state storage costs.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01651v1), 3 August, parallelizes gated-delta tree recurrence and reconstructs only the accepted state from token factors. Unquantized Qwen3.5 4B/9B/27B on A100 and GB10, plus 122B-A10B on four A100s, using native MTP: geometric mean 2.74x AR, 1.26x strongest speculative baseline. GB10 single-user gains over that baseline are 1.11-1.14x. GDN is directly relevant; W4A16/DFlash2 transfer and accessible implementation are UNVERIFIED. Estimate **1-2 days for a kernel prototype**, over a week for full integration. Risk: convolution ancestry, attention masks and state commit must agree. **Verdict: relevant architecture work after the tree feasibility gate.**

8. **DARTree and JetSpec: defer complete ports.** [DARTree](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13524v1), 13 August, reuses an AR correction head to expand branches in batches, then prunes. Qwen3-4B, RTX 6000 Ada, Transformers, batch 1, greedy GSM8K: 12.97 committed tokens and 9.73x local AR. [JetSpec](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.18394), June paper and [22 June blog](https://haoailab.com/blogs/parallel-tree-decoding/), trains causal parallel trees: its serving table reports Qwen3-8B, H100, MATH500, batch 1, 128 nodes, 127.8 AR versus 553.3 tok/s. Neither establishes hybrid W4A16 performance. Estimate **1-3 days for smaller-model reproductions**, over a week here. Risks: absent compatible heads and the same hybrid tree barrier. **Verdict: substantial research gains, weak gain per day for this target.**

New training support is more immediately useful than another recipe: [SpecForge PR 810](https://github.com/sgl-project/SpecForge/pull/810), merged 3 September, adds candidate coverage, oracle/realized paths and expected accepted-length diagnostics. Online values remain training-set proxies. [PR 793](https://github.com/sgl-project/SpecForge/pull/793), merged 31 August, adds selector gradient control. Neither reports a serving speedup. Use these to locate loss before the already-planned continuation work; require held-out serving evaluation after export and requantization.

[July SGLang measurements](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2026-07-06-dspark-sglang/) show dynamic block trimming ties static verification at batch 1 on DeepSeek-V4-Flash/H200 DP4.
